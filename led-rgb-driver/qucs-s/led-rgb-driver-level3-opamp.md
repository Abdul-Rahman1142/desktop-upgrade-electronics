# Level 3: Op-Amp Closed-Loop Current Source

## Status
Not started.

## Planned approach
Add an op-amp in a feedback loop around the current-sensing resistor, so the circuit
actively corrects the current to a precise setpoint regardless of Vf variation between
individual LEDs or temperature drift. This is closed-loop control, a step up from
Level 2's open-loop BJT source.

## What this will demonstrate over Level 2
Precision and temperature stability, feedback control theory applied to a real circuit.
